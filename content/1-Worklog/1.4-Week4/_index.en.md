### Goals

- Develop APIs for students and companies.
- Complete the internship posting and application flow.

### Work Completed

| Task | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Student profile APIs | Create, view, and update profile |
| Company profile APIs | Create, view, and update company information |
| Internship post APIs | Company creates and manages internship posts |
| Application APIs | Student applies to approved internship posts |

### Results

The core business flow was completed: companies can publish internship positions, students can view approved posts, and students can submit applications.
