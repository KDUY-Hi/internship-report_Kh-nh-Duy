## Activities

- Prepared the demo flow from company to admin to student.
- Presented the project problem, solution, and AWS architecture.
- Reviewed improvement points in backend, frontend, and deployment.
- Collected feedback to improve the report.

## Demo Flow

1. Company creates an internship post.
2. Admin approves the post.
3. Student creates a profile, uploads CV, and applies.
4. Company reviews applicants and updates status.
5. Student receives a notification.

## Result

The demo helped the team verify product completeness, identify missing details, and strengthen the presentation of the AWS deployment solution.
